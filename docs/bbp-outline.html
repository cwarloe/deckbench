Audience: trainers and content developers who keep training material in Git

TITLE
Drop Your Files, Get One Markdown Profile
A browser workbench that converts the files in a training repo into Git-friendly Markdown.
ACT 1: SETUP
Setting Role
Your training content lives in decks, handouts, PDFs, Word files, and spreadsheets. You're a trainer or developer who wants it reviewable and versioned in Git.
Point A (Where am I?) Call to Action (How do I get from A to B?) Point B (Where do I want to be?)
Every format needs a different tool, and none of it diffs cleanly. Drop in your files, pick a mode, and download the ZIP. Content you can version, in one Markdown profile.
Transition 1Deckbench puts those formats behind one drop zone. Three ideas explain it: what goes in and out, how output adapts, and how it stays private.
